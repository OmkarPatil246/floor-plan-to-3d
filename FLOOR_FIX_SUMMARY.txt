========================================
  FLOOR TEXTURE COLOR FIX - SUMMARY
========================================

PROBLEM:
--------
❌ Custom floor textures (tiles) appeared with brown/chocolate tint
❌ White tiles looked beige/tan
❌ Grey tiles looked dark brown
❌ All colors were muddy and inaccurate

CAUSE:
------
The floor material had a brown color (0xd4a870) that was being 
MULTIPLIED with the uploaded texture, causing a brown tint.

In Three.js: Final Color = Texture Color × Material Color

SOLUTION:
---------
Changed floor material color from BROWN to WHITE (0xffffff)

White × Any Color = Same Color (no tint!)

CHANGES:
--------
1. Initial floor material: color: 0xffffff ✓
2. Texture reset function: color: 0xffffff ✓  
3. Custom upload handler: color: 0xffffff ✓
